# Introduction

**CCC Guide** is a free guide for the [Canadian Computing Competition](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/computing) (CCC), covering both the **Junior** and **Senior** divisions. It is grounded in an empirical study of every CCC problem from 2012–2026.
