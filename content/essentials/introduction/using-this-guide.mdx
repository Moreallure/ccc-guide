# Using This Guide

Welcome to the CCC Guide — a **free, open-source** resource for the Canadian Computing Competition, covering everything from your first Junior contest to the path toward CCO.

Problems are labelled by level and question number: **J1–J5** for Junior and **S1–S5** for Senior.

## Practice and further resources

You can submit your solutions to [DMOJ](https://dmoj.ca/) and have them judged against the full test data, the same way the CCC grades them. Every problem covered in this guide links to its original statement on the CEMC site and to its DMOJ page. This project was also inspired by the [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide/), which is a good place to continue once you move beyond the CCC, especially for advanced topics in C++.

## Contribute

This guide is open source and improved through community contributions: fixing explanations, adding new content, and improving the curriculum. The source for every page is available at [github.com/Moreallure/ccc-guide](https://github.com/Moreallure/ccc-guide) — pull requests are welcome.
