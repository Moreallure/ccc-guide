# What is the CCC?

**CCC Guide** is a free, open-source guide for the
[Canadian Computing Competition](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/computing),
built by the community and grounded in an empirical study of every CCC
problem from 2012–2026.

## How judging works

<Callout title="Contest format">
  5 problems per division, 15 marks each, 3 hours. Partial credit is awarded
  per subtask — strategy matters as much as algorithms.
</Callout>
